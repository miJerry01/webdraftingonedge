TRENCHING AND CONDUIT: GENERAL NOTES

Before excavation

  1. Request utility locates (811) and field-verify marked lines before digging (29 CFR 1926.651(b)).
  2. Verify the conduit route, bend locations, and equipment pad positions against the construction drawings.
  3. Do not excavate within [3 ft] of a guy anchor rod or under energized or owner-installed equipment unless owner personnel are present.

Excavation safety (29 CFR 1926 Subpart P)

  1. A competent person must inspect the excavation daily and after any rain, vibration, or change in conditions (1926.651(k)).
  2. Excavations 5 ft or deeper require a protective system (sloping, benching, shoring, or shielding) unless cut in stable rock (1926.652). Excavations over 20 ft require a registered professional engineer's design.
  3. Keep spoil and equipment at least 2 ft from the edge (1926.651(j)(2)). Place spoil on the side opposite the access point where practical.
  4. Provide a ladder or ramp within 25 ft of lateral travel for any trench 4 ft or deeper (1926.651(c)(2)).
  5. Control water accumulation, and test the atmosphere where a hazardous atmosphere is reasonably expected (1926.651(g), (h)).

Conduit installation

  1. Trench depth: [36 in] minimum for service runs, or as required by NESC Rule 352 and the project drawings.
  2. Conduit: PVC Schedule 40 meeting ASTM D1785, with fittings to ASTM D2466. Do not use other conduit types where connecting to existing equipment.
  3. Bends must not be tighter than the cable manufacturer's minimum bending radius or the NEC minimum, whichever is larger.
  4. Install service conduit at the same time as primary and secondary conduit. Tie off and cap all conduit ends at equipment locations before requesting inspection.
  5. Provide a [2 ft x 5 ft] bell hole when modifying existing cable-in-conduit.
