15kV INSULATOR CATALOG NO.A111

KEY FEATURES AND SPECIFICATIONS:

  • Application: Specifically for riser poles to protect underground cables.
  • Voltage Rating: 18 KV nominal.
  • MCOV: 15.3 kV.
  • Construction: Polymer housing (often ESP™ or silicone) for superior tracking resistance and durability.
  • Components: Usually includes a mounting bracket and sometimes a disconnect switch.
  • Performance: Designed to handle high energy discharge with lower let-through voltage compared to standard distribution arresters.
